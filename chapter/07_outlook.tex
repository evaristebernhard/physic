\section{Scope and further questions}\label{sec:outlook}

The exact computation of $\beta_3$ and \Cref{thm:finite-Z} complete the finite--$Z$ propagation of the cubic HPS mean--field constant through the presently available finite--configuration and weighted kinetic estimates.  The two conclusions should be kept conceptually separate.  The first is an exact solution of the scalar radial mean--field variational problem.  The second is an explicit atomic bound obtained by inserting that exact constant into the existing HPS many--body framework and reoptimizing the finite--$Z$ bookkeeping.

The resulting coefficient
\[
 \beta_3^{-1}=1.086325175\ldots
\]
is therefore the coefficient delivered by the present cubic mean--field input together with the unchanged HPS finite--$N$ estimates.  We do not claim that it is optimal among all weighted multiplier arguments, and we do not prove the ionization conjecture $N_c(Z)\le Z+C$.

The explicit extremizer nevertheless identifies the geometry responsible for the remaining scalar mean--field obstruction: in logarithmic radius it is a compactly supported cosine profile, and in physical space its density is proportional to
\[
 r^{-4}\cos(\sqrt2\log r)
\]
on a finite annulus.  Any improvement beyond the coefficient obtained here must therefore use information that is absent from the scalar radial variational problem, for example genuinely many--fermion information or additional positive terms retained from the weighted multiplier identity.  We do not pursue such extensions in the present paper.
